# Who to meet

Goal: Book first meetings with fraud and financial-crimes leaders at banks, credit unions and payments companies at the Midway Risk Summit.

## Segment: aml-financial-crimes
- **Who:** BSA/AML officers, heads of financial crimes, AML / transaction-monitoring directors, chief compliance officers at banks and credit unions (roughly USD 5B to 150B in assets). Not analysts, not consultants, not regulators.
- **Why they matter:** buyer
- **Cares about:** alert backlogs and false positives, investigator staffing, exam findings on monitoring, consent orders and lookbacks, coverage gaps after a core migration.
- **How to write:** brisk and plain, under 80 words, one number and one direct ask. Proof that lands: Northfield Credit Union (named) and the anonymous top-25 bank. No marketing words. They read "AI" claims as spam.
- **Ask:** 20 minutes at Tallyline booth 512
- **Research sources:** bank press releases, enforcement actions and consent orders, conference speaker bios, LinkedIn posts (read only)
- **Must meet:** Merriton Bank financial-crimes leadership

## Segment: fraud-leaders
- **Who:** heads, VPs and directors of fraud, fraud strategy, payments risk, disputes and chief risk officers at banks and credit unions. Not fraud analysts, not branch staff.
- **Why they matter:** buyer
- **Cares about:** scam losses on instant and P2P payments, check fraud on mobile deposit, member reimbursement, alert volume after an RTP or FedNow launch, two queues for fraud and AML.
- **How to write:** brisk, under 80 words, one number, direct ask. Sterling Rock Bank (USD 4.1M stopped) is the proof that lands with fraud people.
- **Ask:** 20 minutes at Tallyline booth 512
- **Research sources:** bank news, conference talks, payments-industry press
- **Must meet:** Merriton Bank fraud leadership

## Segment: fintech-payments-risk
- **Who:** heads of risk, fraud or trust & safety at payments companies, card issuers/processors and neobanks (1M+ transactions a day).
- **Why they matter:** buyer
- **Cares about:** approval rates versus fraud losses, scoring latency at peak, disputes on new merchants, sponsor-bank expectations, mule accounts.
- **How to write:** brisk and technical, under 80 words, one number. Proof: the anonymous payments fintech (45 ms p99) only. One number, not a stack of latency stats.
- **Ask:** 20 minutes at Tallyline booth 512
- **Research sources:** company blogs, funding news, sponsor-bank announcements
