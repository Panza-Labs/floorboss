# Voice

- **Tone:** brisk and direct, salesy but concrete. One number, one ask.
- **Length:** under 80 words for a first message; follow-ups two or three sentences.
- **Greeting:** "Hi <first name>,"
- **Sign-off:** "Marcus"
- **Sender name:** Marcus Bell, Senior Enterprise AE, Tallyline · +1 (212) 555-0148 · 1 Example Plaza, New York, NY
- **Humour:** none
- **Numbers:** one number per email: lead with it, and use it in the subject when it reads. A second number only if it is the proof that names Tallyline.
- **Avoid:** AI-powered, revolutionary, unlock, synergy, game-changer, "free pilot", any pricing, "just checking in"
