# What you bring

**In one sentence:** Tallyline is a fraud and transaction-monitoring platform for banks, credit unions and payments companies: real-time risk scores on card, ACH, wire, P2P and RTP/FedNow payments, AML transaction monitoring with tunable scenarios, and one case queue for fraud and AML investigators.

**Problems it solves, per segment**
- aml-financial-crimes: "Our investigators drown in false-positive alerts and examiners keep finding gaps in monitoring."
- fraud-leaders: "Scam losses on instant payments keep climbing and fraud and AML work the same cases twice."
- fintech-payments-risk: "We need to score every payment fast enough not to hurt approvals."

**What they get from the meeting:** how peers cut alert volume and handling time, and how a 90-day rollout runs (shadow mode on historical data in weeks 1-4, side-by-side in weeks 5-8, cutover by scenario from week 9).
