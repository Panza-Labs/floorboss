# Who to meet

Goal: investor meetings for Lanecraft's seed round at Harbour & Haul Expo.

## Segment: Seed investors
- **Who:** partners and principals at venture funds that invest in logistics or supply-chain software at seed or Series A.
- **Why they matter:** investor.
- **Cares about:** traction, why now, the size of the carrier-onboarding problem.
- **How to write:** founder to investor, brief and specific, under 90 words. One traction number, no deck attachments.
- **Ask:** a 30-minute coffee at the expo
- **Research sources:** fund portfolio pages, partner blog posts

## Segment: Strategic investors
- **Who:** venture or corporate development leads at large shipping and logistics groups.
- **Why they matter:** investor and potential design partner.
- **Cares about:** whether their business units could pilot it.
- **How to write:** formal, short, focused on a pilot path, under 90 words.
- **Ask:** a 30-minute meeting at the expo
- **Research sources:** corporate venture announcements
