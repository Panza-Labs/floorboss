# Who to meet

Goal: meetings with freight and logistics buyers at Harbour & Haul Expo.

## Segment: Ops leaders
- **Who:** VP, Director or Head of Operations, Network Operations or Carrier Operations at freight, logistics, 3PL and shipping companies. COOs at haulage companies.
- **Why they matter:** buyer. They own carrier onboarding and feel the delay when a new lane needs carriers.
- **Cares about:** opening new lanes faster, carrier compliance, fewer manual checks.
- **How to write:** direct and practical, under 100 words, no hype. Numbers land; peer company names land.
- **Ask:** a 30-minute meeting at the expo
- **Research sources:** company news, conference talks, hiring posts

## Segment: Procurement
- **Who:** Heads or Directors of Procurement, category managers for freight or transport, at shippers (retail, grocery, pharma, manufacturing).
- **Why they matter:** buyer or strong influencer when transport is re-tendered.
- **Cares about:** carrier compliance during tenders, supplier risk, documentation.
- **How to write:** formal and precise, under 90 words. They read a lot of vendor email; be specific in the first line.
- **Ask:** a 30-minute meeting at the expo
- **Research sources:** tender announcements, procurement forum talks

## Segment: Carrier relations
- **Who:** Heads of carrier relations, carrier partnerships or carrier management at brokers and forwarders.
- **Why they matter:** influencer; they run the carrier network day to day.
- **Cares about:** carrier experience, faster payouts, less paperwork for hauliers.
- **How to write:** friendly, short, peer to peer, under 80 words.
- **Ask:** 20 minutes
- **Research sources:** company news, network size announcements
