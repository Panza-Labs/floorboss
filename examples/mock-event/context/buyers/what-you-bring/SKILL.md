# What you bring

**In one sentence:** Lanecraft is carrier onboarding software for freight companies: it collects and checks a new carrier's documents, insurance and compliance in one flow, so a carrier can run its first load in days instead of weeks.

**Problems it solves, per segment**
- Ops leaders: "a new lane waits two weeks for carriers to clear onboarding."
- Procurement: "we can't prove every carrier in a tender is compliant."
- Carrier relations: "hauliers drop out because our paperwork takes too long."

**What they get from the meeting:** a 20-minute look at their current onboarding steps against a benchmark from similar fleets, and a demo of the compliance check.

**What you know that they don't**

| insight | basis | segments | situations |
|---|---|---|---|

| Asking for compliance documents at bid stage roughly halves carrier drop-out after a tender is awarded | two retail tenders run on Lanecraft | procurement | tender, re-tender, consolidation |
| Hauliers who wait more than a week for onboarding take a third fewer loads in their first month | across 2,000 onboarded carriers | carrier-relations, ops-leaders | growth, network expansion |
| Temperature-controlled carriers fail compliance checks twice as often, usually on certificate expiry dates | Lanecraft check data, 2026 | procurement, ops-leaders | cold chain, pharma, food |
| Carriers who can upload documents from a phone finish onboarding in about half the time of email-and-PDF flows | across 2,000 onboarded carriers | carrier-relations | growth, network expansion |
| In their first quarter, hauliers name slow first payment more often than paperwork as the reason they stop taking loads | haulier survey, 2026, 400 responses | carrier-relations, ops-leaders | growth, retention |
| About one in five new carriers is held up by a single missing document, most often a vehicle licence scan | Lanecraft check data, 2026 | carrier-relations, ops-leaders | new lane, growth |
| Tenders that score compliance alongside price lose about a quarter fewer carriers in the first six months | three retail and grocery tenders run on Lanecraft | procurement | tender, re-tender |
| Mid-contract insurance lapses are usually found at a claim, not at renewal: about 4 in 10 lapses surfaced that way | across Lanecraft's 14 customers, 2026 | procurement, ops-leaders | consolidation, cold chain, tender |

**Alternatives:** spreadsheets and email, or the onboarding module in a TMS. The honest difference: Lanecraft does only onboarding and compliance, so it's faster to set up but doesn't replace the TMS.

**Never say:** "AI-powered", "end-to-end platform", any price, anything about competitors by name.
